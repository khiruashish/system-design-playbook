    Industry Designs¶

    What this tab is

    These are end-to-end system designs for real sectors, each in the handbook's 9-part format:

    • requirements and estimates;
    • architecture with happy-path and failure-path diagrams;
    • a design-doc excerpt;
    • four runnable labs with recorded outputs;
    • production concerns and anti-patterns;
    • 20 interview questions.

    Each design builds on the core chapters rather than repeating them.

    From I04 onwards the designs are system walkthroughs with no code. They explain in detail how the system works end to end: journeys, subsystems, algorithms, the data platform and warehouse, and the tech stack. I01 and I02 have companion walkthrough pages (I01b, I02b) in the same style.

    Company-named designs are "in the style of" and based on public sources. They are not descriptions of any company's internal systems. Regulatory statements are marked VERIFY.
