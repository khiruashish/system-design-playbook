B. Where each retriever fails (120-word chunks): recall@5 by question type
   question type        BM25   embeddings (toy)           hybrid RRF
   exact-id (20)       100%                0%                100%

   product-name (10)      100%              100%                100%

   -> hybrid search over 120-word chunks puts the complete answer in the prompt for 93% of questions.
     BM25 nails exact identifiers (SKU codes) and misses paraphrases; embeddings do the opposite.
     Fusing ranks (RRF) needs no score calibration and keeps both strengths.
     Tiny chunks split the answer: the right page is found but half the facts never reach the model.
     Huge chunks carry the whole answer but multiply prompt tokens (cost and time to first token) and bury it in noise.
